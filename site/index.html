Week 2: LLM inference performance

Follow prefill and decode, explain weight and KV-cache traffic, and see how batching, PagedAttention, and FlashAttention address different bottlenecks. The single-GPU core leads to a preview of separate prefill and decode GPU pools and their KV-transfer tradeoff.

Week 3: Scaling LLMs across GPUs

Follow tensors across GPUs to understand data, tensor, pipeline, context, and expert parallelism. Worked examples connect each partition to the communication it requires, then compare serving layouts and ZeRO/FSDP training-state budgets. Additional lessons cover pipeline bubbles and microbatch choices, TP/CP constraints, and communication-aware placement within and across nodes.

Week 4: LLM serving

Start with speculative decoding: draft several tokens, verify them with the target model, and understand when the extra work reduces generation time. Then follow a four-GPU chat service through prefix reuse, queues, scheduling, routing, deployment, and controlled load testing.

Present slides33 slides · expanded teaching deck

Slides 2–3 introduce speculative decoding and exact sampling, followed by KV continuation and an interactive timing comparison (4–5). Slides 6–7 pose a multiple-choice sampling quiz and explain the solution. Slides 8–11 develop the theory: exact sampling, distribution overlap, expected tokens per round, and the speedup tradeoff.
